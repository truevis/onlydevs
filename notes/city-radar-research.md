# City Radar Research

Playful 0–100 scores for Onlydevs City Radar axes:
**Lightning talks · Coffee strength · Late-night hacking · Wi‑Fi reliability · Networking density**

Scores are research-informed vibes, not surveys. Updated 2026-09-17.

---

## Bangkok

**Scene:** Long-running Only Devs (ex-ThaiPy) pairs two short talks with open-mic **5‑minute lightnings**, then hangout. BKK/hack keeps monthly hands-on nights in Thong Lo. Ari café density is a nomad village; The Hive Thonglor is the grown-up fiber backup (24/7, ~100–200 Mbps).

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 92 | Only Devs formalizes capped 5‑min community lightnings after main talks |
| Coffee strength | 90 | Ari specialty density (Factory, Nine One, drips) + city café culture |
| Late-night hacking | 84 | BKK/hack evenings; Hive 24/7 for US/EU hours |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 82 | Cowork/condo fiber strong; café Wi‑Fi still lottery |
| Networking density | 88 | Only Devs + BKK/hack + AI builder cafés keep Sukhumvit busy |

**Sources / notes**
- https://www.meetup.com/only_devs_bangkok/
- https://www.meetup.com/bkkhack/
- https://thehive.co.th/
- https://www.earthsims.com/country/bangkok-digital-nomad-guide/

**Proposed values:** `[92, 90, 84, 82, 88]`

## Austin

**Scene:** SXSW and Capital Factory concentrate pitches, mini-hacks, and startup crawl density. Austin Code and Coffee + Vibe Code keep laptop mornings/afternoons alive; Station / CF nights stretch late during festival season more than as a year-round ritual.

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 86 | SXSW-style pitches + recurring AI mini-hack demos |
| Coffee strength | 80 | Code & Coffee culture, cold brew on tap—solid, not Vienna |
| Late-night hacking | 82 | Capital Factory / festival nights; weekday sessions often end earlier |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 94 | US cowork + café infra is rarely the blocker |
| Networking density | 96 | BBQ-line density during SXSW; CF as year-round gravity well |

**Sources / notes**
- https://www.meetup.com/austin-code-and-coffee/
- https://www.meetup.com/vibe-code-austin/
- https://aitx.beehiiv.com/p/austin-meetup-recap-hack-fair-this-weekend-ai-house-sxsw
- Capital Factory / SXSW coverage (e.g. cisco.com DevNet @ Capital Factory)

**Proposed values:** `[86, 80, 82, 94, 96]`

## Berlin

**Scene:** Berlin Hack & Tell at c-base is sacred: **5 minutes show + 5 minutes roast**, no business pitch. Deep night culture around hackerspaces and build nights; café Wi‑Fi in Altbau courtyards still ghosts you.

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 96 | BHNT format is the lightning-talk archetype |
| Coffee strength | 88 | Strong third-wave scene across Mitte/Kreuzberg/etc. |
| Late-night hacking | 97 | c-base / OpenHack-style deep nights are cultural |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 70 | Famous café Wi‑Fi roulette between courtyards |
| Networking density | 80 | Huge scene, more distributed than single-hub cities |

**Sources / notes**
- https://www.meetup.com/berlin-hack-and-tell/
- https://bhnt.c-base.org
- https://github.com/berlin-hack-and-tell

## Da Nang

**Scene:** Vietnam coffee capital vibes on the beach strip. Frontier Club runs AI meetups + Founder Hub cowork at Local Beans / Webe; AI Tinkerers Da Nang is demo-first. An Thuong cafés + ACE cowork (open late, ethernet 800+ Mbps) punch above skyline size—intimate density, not mega-meetup scale.

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 72 | Growing talk/demo culture (Frontier Club, AI Tinkerers)—not yet BHNT-scale |
| Coffee strength | 97 | Cà phê sữa đá + An Thuong specialty/roaster cafés |
| Late-night hacking | 78 | ACE ~2am + US-timezone-friendly hours; fewer classic midnight hackathons |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 88 | Viettel fiber common; cowork ethernet excellent; cafés usually fine |
| Networking density | 76 | Tight founder/dev circle; smaller absolute crowd than BKK/Austin |

**Sources / notes**
- https://frontierclub.ai/
- https://luma.com/v09x4904 (Frontier Club AI Meetup)
- https://luma.com/xf1krpmo (Founder Hub @ Webe Coffee)
- https://www.dananghotelguide.com/da-nang-digital-nomad-guide.html
- https://hitchhive.app/coworking-spaces-cafes-da-nang/

**Proposed values:** `[72, 97, 78, 88, 76]`

## Lagos

**Scene:** GDG Lagos and DevFest Lagos (14th edition / multi-day) are continent-scale energy. Yaba (“Yabacon Valley”) clusters hubs where fiber + generators are a lifestyle; outside them, bandwidth is still a side quest. Ambition and networking density outrun café culture.

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 88 | DevFest / GDG talk tracks and community sessions |
| Coffee strength | 62 | Functional fuel, not a destination coffee city |
| Late-night hacking | 94 | Buildathons and hub culture don’t do half-speed |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 52 | Fiber rollout improving (~4,000 km claimed) but uneven outside hubs |
| Networking density | 97 | Massive, hungry developer community |

**Sources / notes**
- https://gdglagos.com/
- https://devfestlagos.com/
- https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigerias-startup-capital-lays-4000km-of-fibre-to-boost-tech-ambitions/
- https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/valleys-hubs-yaba-phenomenon-foundersconnectshow-jub7f

**Proposed values:** `[88, 62, 94, 52, 97]`

## Singapore

**Scene:** AI Tinkerers is demo-first; Hackware and HackerspaceSG (Textile Centre, 24/7 members) hold the after-hours line. Hackapura is **daytime** indie-hacker shipping (Thu 10–17). Fiber is basically a utility; kopi-o and third-wave both win.

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 86 | AI Tinkerers demos + Hackware / community show-and-tells |
| Coffee strength | 82 | Kopi culture + specialty—strong everyday fuel |
| Late-night hacking | 55 | Daytime Hackapura bias; late nights live at the hackerspace / rare all-nighters |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 99 | National fiber / mobile quality is a non-issue |
| Networking density | 84 | Dense, structured meetups; less chaotic street energy |

**Sources / notes**
- https://hackerspace.sg/
- https://www.hackapura.com/
- https://singapore.aitinkerers.org/

**Proposed values:** `[86, 82, 55, 99, 84]`

## Toronto

**Scene:** TorontoJS TechTalks (talks + networking), Coffee & Code / Dupont Morning Code at Creeds, Double Down co-hacking (~17–21), Build Night TO demos. Third-wave coffee is elite; true overnight laptop havens are rare outside pop-ups (e.g. Late-Night Coffeeshop).

| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|------|------:|-----------|
| Lightning talks | 78 | Solid TechTalks/demo nights; less lightning-ritualized than BHNT/Only Devs |
| Coffee strength | 92 | Creeds + city third-wave bench |
| Late-night hacking | 72 | Double Down / Build Night evenings; overnight spaces uncommon |
| Wi‑Fi reliability | 90 | Cowork + café connectivity generally trustworthy |
| Networking density | 82 | Large TorontoJS / builder Slack + recurring cowork |

**Sources / notes**
- https://www.meetup.com/torontojs/
- https://www.meetup.com/coffee-code/

**Proposed values:** `[78, 92, 72, 90, 82]`
